## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide editorial banner for physician Kaisar Dauyey's Clinical Practice page. Primary request: original artistic image about general practice, whole person care and continuity in Kazakhstan. Create a sophisticated hand painted natural history journal aesthetic, watercolor and fine etched pencil on warm ivory textured paper, evergreen, teal, muted ochre and gentle terracotta, matching an elegant research website. Wide panoramic 3:1 composition. On the right half, a male physician with dark short hair seen in three quarter rear view sits at the same eye level across a simple desk from an adult patient in everyday clothes, listening attentively in a calm sunlit outpatient clinic. The figures are fictional illustrations, no claim of real patient encounter or identifiable portrait. Modest stethoscope and closed clinical notebook on the desk, no instruments in use, no screen full of invented medical data. Light through a window with a subtle southern Kazakhstan mountain landscape. Delicate overlapping translucent paper contours suggest repeated visits and the passage of time without duplicate people. Leave the left half mostly quiet, deep evergreen watercolor wash suitable for a large white heading and short quote overlaid separately in HTML. Tender yet unsentimental, professional and humane, artistically rich, no stock photo look. No text, labels, typography, logos, medical charts, syringe, dramatic emergency, red cross symbols, flags, watermarks or exaggerated smiles.
