# Writing and structure

The October 2026 redesign follows the supplied paper collection and the final 2024 doctoral thesis. Existing Huxley quotations and source links remain unchanged.

## Visitor route

Home introduces the author and gives visitors four research questions. Research provides section links and fuller explanations. Publications retains the chronological record and adds a guide by subject. CV and Contact complete the main navigation. Clinical Practice and Diagnostic Medicine remain available through Home and the research pages.
