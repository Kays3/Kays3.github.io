## Validation

Jekyll build and all 40 language routes passed. Checked reciprocal language switching, alternate language metadata, navigation destinations, local media files, matched banner settings, publication DOI sets and links to the two private current research repositories. No private repository links were found on the audited pages. Desktop and mobile checks cover all four languages on CV, Diagnostic Medicine and Clinical Practice.

Publication titles and author records, institutional names, technical method names and Huxley quotations retain their original language deliberately. This audit checks site consistency, not independent certification of credentials, dates or every scientific claim.
