# Home intro animation artwork

Used by `assets/js/home-anemone.js` on the home page. The scene illustrates a hypothesis and does not show data.

* `reef-backdrop.webp`: reef scene with a bubble-tip anemone and a carpet anemone. AI-generated with Canva image generation, 10 November 2026.
* `reef-animals.webp`, tomato clownfish, pink skunk clownfish and anemone shrimp: AI-generated with Canva image generation, 10 November 2026, then cut out from a white background.
* `reef-animals.webp`, ocellaris clownfish, Clark's anemonefish and three-spot damsel: the clean side profiles from `assets/fish-illustrations/` (TE Mobilome Paper illustrations), mirrored and resized. Their sources and terms are in `assets/fish-illustrations/CREDITS.md`.

Motion (swaying, swimming, the transposon blocks and the algae cells) is drawn by the script. AI-generated images are simplified and may not match species markings exactly.
