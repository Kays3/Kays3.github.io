<section class="intro-block" aria-labelledby="about-heading">
  <p class="eyebrow">About</p>
  <h2 id="about-heading">Biology is connected across scales. My work is too.</h2>
  <p>I am a computational biologist and scientific workflow developer, currently a postdoctoral researcher at Hokkaido University. My work moves between biological AI, single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, evolutionary genomics, computational neuroscience, and environmental adaptation.</p>
  <p>I am especially interested in making complex analyses easier to test, reproduce, review, and extend. That means pairing biological questions with explicit inputs and outputs, validation checkpoints, portable environments, and documentation that lets the next person understand not only <em>what</em> ran, but <em>why</em>.</p>
</section>
