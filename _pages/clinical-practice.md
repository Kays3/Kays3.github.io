<p class="page-intro">General practice is where I meet the whole person first. Before a diagnosis has a name, before a specialist is involved, someone walks in with a worry. I love being the physician who hears it first and stays with it.</p>

<section class="specialty-hero specialty-hero--family" aria-labelledby="family-why-title">
  <div class="specialty-hero__copy">
    <p class="eyebrow">Clinical practice</p>
    <h2 id="family-why-title">Undifferentiated problems, long relationships, and care that fits real life</h2>
    <p>In general practice I saw adults with symptoms that did not yet belong to any specialty. Sorting them out means listening well, examining carefully, choosing the few tests that matter, and following up. It also means coordinating with other specialists so the patient does not carry that burden alone.</p>
    <p>That work taught me to respect uncertainty and to value continuity. A result that looks clean in a paper is only useful if it fits a ten minute visit, a family's circumstances, and the resources of the clinic.</p>

<section class="specialty-themes" aria-labelledby="family-themes-title">
  <div class="section-heading"><p class="eyebrow">What I care about</p><h2 id="family-themes-title">Four habits from the clinic</h2></div>
  <div class="interest-grid">
    <article class="interest-card"><p class="eyebrow">Whole person</p><h2>Start with the story</h2><p>A presenting complaint sits inside a life: work, family, money, fear. Adult outpatient care means assessing the disease and the person at the same time, then planning treatment that both can carry.</p></article>
    <article class="interest-card"><p class="eyebrow">Continuity</p><h2>Follow up is part of the diagnosis</h2><p>Many answers only appear on the second or third visit. I document carefully so the next encounter, with me or with a colleague, begins where the last one ended.</p></article>
    <article class="interest-card"><p class="eyebrow">Missed diagnoses</p><h2>Take young patients seriously</h2><p>My first case report described a young woman whose colorectal cancer was diagnosed late after childbirth. I keep that case in mind whenever a symptom is easy to explain away.</p><p><a class="text-link" href="https://nur.nu.edu.kz/items/87716040-78c3-4c47-a842-906c5421cf60">Read the case report <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span></a></p></article>
    <article class="interest-card"><p class="eyebrow">Care of the carers</p><h2>The workforce is part of the system</h2><p>With colleagues in Kazakhstan I studied burnout, emotional strain, and denial among palliative care professionals. Good primary and palliative care depends on people who are themselves supported.</p><p><a class="text-link" href="https://doi.org/10.1186/s12904-025-01798-z">Read the palliative care study <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span></a></p></article>
